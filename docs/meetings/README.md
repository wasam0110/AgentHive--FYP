# Meeting records

Meet weekly as directed by the policy and supervisor. Record actual discussions, individual progress and next actions. The policy also states at least 15 meetings with at least three days between meetings; confirm scheduling because its stated window is inconsistent. Fortnightly signoff is a separate requirement.

Use MOM-template.md as a blank working source and the original DOCX for the signed submission. Never prefill attendance, signatures or meeting outcomes from a planned work session. Keep completed private forms in submissions/private/, which is ignored by Git.
